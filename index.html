The gap

The problem

Every clinician-researcher hits the same wall: a dataset, and no way to analyse it rigorously without learning to program or waiting weeks for a statistician. The existing options each fail on something that matters.

01

R packages

tableone, rms, tidymodels are excellent — and assume you can code. That's the barrier we remove.

02

Cloud AutoML

Powerful, but sends data off-site and isn't built for clinical reporting. A non-starter for patient data.

03

Fixed calculators

NSQIP and friends are validated on someone else's population and can't be retrained on your cohort.

How it works

How it works

STEP 01

Load your data

Drop in a CSV or export. ClinTab-ML profiles missingness, distributions, and variable types — no cleaning script required.

STEP 02

Run the workflow

Baseline table, imputation, survival analysis, and up to 15 models with proper splits — every one reported, calibration included.

STEP 03

Export the paper's plumbing

Publication-DPI ROC and calibration plots, an auto-written methods paragraph, and a TRIPOD+AI coverage report.

Rigor by default

→
Reports every model tried, not just the winning AUC — no cherry-picking.
→
Calibration shown by default, alongside discrimination.
→
Warns when events-per-variable is too low to model honestly.
→
Enforces clean train / test splits and flags leakage.
→
Emits a reproducibility bundle: seed, splits, versions, config.
→
Outputs shaped to TRIPOD+AI so your paper starts compliant.
